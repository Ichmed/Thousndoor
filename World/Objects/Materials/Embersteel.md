Wrought iron, twisted by blacksmiths that have studied the alchemical knowledge of the [[Burning Church]]. Embersteel has the ability to manifest a continuous holy flame on it own, giving it a large holy as well as profane significance.

While the rest of [[Thousndoor]] is lit by [[Laubgas]] lanterns, areas occupied by the church rely solely on [[Embersteel]] as a source of light.
