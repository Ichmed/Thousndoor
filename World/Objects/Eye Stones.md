These mysterious menhirs are rumored to have been created by the [[Lost Kingdom of Gurash]] and lie strewn throughout the [[Northern Highlands]] and the [[Folded Fjords]]. Their surface is covered in eye-like carvings, though these "eyes" are not contained within a circular socket, instead their shape spirals outward, merging with other shapes and creating a fingerprint like pattern.

When sleeping near these monuments, dreamers will always enter the [[Domain of the Watcher]]
