A tithe paid by members of the [[Burning Church]] and their subjects. It is exclusively paid with coins hammered from the [[Granalia of Attonement]].

The [[Iron Debt]] is paid by every faithful, from the lowest wrech to nobility and clergy, though well to be individuals will most often purchase their coins instead of hammering them themselves.

These coins are then molten and used in a multitude of rituals and industrial processes like the crateion of [[Embersteel]]
