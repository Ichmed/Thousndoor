The traditional funeral rights of seafarers in the northern [[Bittermeer]], and following from that all Members and associates of the [[Sorka Fleet]], consists of wrapping the body in white linnen, ropes, and chains and then tying a heavy weight around the feet of the deceased. The corpse is then thrown overboard, sinking to the dark murky depths of the ocean.
Though the name suggest that anchors are always used as weights, this is only true for ship captains and other high ranking individuals. Most commoners are simply sunk wrapped in linnen and tied to a rock. Very wealthy individuals on the other hand are enveloped in fine silk and gilded chains, before they are dragged to their final resting place by giant anchors, constructed of marble, precious metals and gemstones.
